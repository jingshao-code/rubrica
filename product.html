The product

Consistent, private, and explained.

Rubrica is a grading model you run inside your own institution. It scores the same way every time, shows the evidence behind each grade, and gives students feedback they can use.

How it works

Step 1

Bring your rubric

Upload your existing rubric and a handful of graded examples for the assignment.

Step 2

We adapt the model

An open weight model is tuned for your subject so it grades to your standard, not a generic one.

Step 3

Deploy on your side

It runs on your servers or private cloud. Nothing about a student leaves your network.

Step 4

Grade with evidence

Each score arrives with its reasoning and feedback, ready for faculty to review.

What makes the grade trustworthy

01

It stays consistent

Answers that mean the same thing get the same score, however they are worded or ordered.

02

It reads the evidence

The model is guided to the parts of an answer that matter, not length, style, or filler text.

03

It stays on campus

Self hosted on your own GPUs, so you keep control of student data and stay FERPA aligned.

A grade should explain itself

Other tools return a number and stop. Rubrica returns three things.

✓

The score

A consistent grade against your rubric.

🔎

The rationale

Which criteria were met, and the exact passage each judgment rests on, so faculty can check and defend it.

✎

The feedback

Specific notes on what to improve, so students learn from the grade instead of just receiving it.

See it on your own assignments

We will run a short pilot on a real assignment from your course and show you the scores, the reasoning, and the feedback.
